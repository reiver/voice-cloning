Voice Cloning

synthetic replica of a specific person's voice which can be used to make it synthetically say anything desired

by Charles Iliya Krempeaux

Voice Cloning is a process of creating a synthetic replica of a specific person's voice which can be used to make it synthetically say anything desired

It has a number of uses, such as creating marketing martials.

2018 June
Transfer Learning from Speaker Verification to Multispeaker Text-To-Speech Synthesis
notes:

  • transfer learning:

    A model trained to recognize speakers can also learn a powerful representation of speaker identity. That representation can be reused to make a TTS system speak in the voice of people it has never seen before.

  • Before this work, most multi-speaker TTS systems tended to have the following limitation:

    • They could only synthesize speech for speakers that were part of their training.
    • To make it so the model could synthesize speech for another speaker, you often needed a lot of transcribed speech.
    • Training a new voice was expensive.

  • zero-shot voice cloning:

    Now, with only a few seconds of audio from a previously unseen speaker, the model is able to generate arbitrary text in that speaker's voice.

  • The following is the structure of the model:

    Each component is trained separately.

    Three separate artificial neural networks are combied.

  • This paper showed that:

    • Speaker identity can be learned separately from TTS.
    • Speaker verification embeddings transfer well to speech synthesis.
    • Zero-shot voice cloning is practical.
    • Large speaker datasets are more valuable than previously thought.
